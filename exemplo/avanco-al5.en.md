# How Far We’ve Reached with AL5

Real measurement of Nei Maldaner’s (INEMA) work with nivel-ia itself, on 11/17/2026, over the last 30 days. Totals only: no requests, folders, clients, channels, or machines.

## The picture

A large part of the operation already runs at **AL5**: research, scheduled routines, data updates, consolidation, monitoring, content production, and video generation.

These workflows run on their own, with an orchestrator, specialized agents, workers, result checks, error alerts, full logging, and a way to recover when something fails.

The scale is already substantial:

- **97,066 automatic runs** across 62 scheduled routines.
- **99.9% accuracy**.
- **919 hours of work returned** through automation.
- **963 automatic publications**.
- Research, trend radar, content creation, updates, reports, and video generation running as recurring workflows.

The main point: AL5 is no longer just a one-off task. It is the **end-to-end operation running on its own**: it executes, checks the result, detects failures, and keeps running with minimal human involvement.

## Why AL5 looks smaller than it is

The main figure is weighted by **time**. An automatic routine lasts about 1 minute; a conversation with Nei lasts about 40. Counted by **session**, AL5 is almost everything:

Level | Share of sessions | Share of time | Minutes per session
--- | --- | --- | ---
AL3 | 2.8% | 37.2% | 41.7
AL4 | 2.5% | 29.5% | 37.4
**AL5** | **94.1%** | **31.2%** | **1.0**

Of 17,937 sessions during the month, 16,928 ran on their own and 1,009 were conversations. That’s why the certificate has two pages: the ladder (time with AI) and the automatic work (routines, runs, accuracy, and hours returned).

## What’s still missing (measured, not hidden)

- **Producing videos** (the largest task, ~73 h/week) is already at AL4 and runs on its own 91% of the time. It’s the next candidate for AL5, with a gain of up to +13 points. It needs 50 entries on the form to prove its accuracy.
- **Routine accuracy** has been measured (99.9%). **Conversation accuracy** hasn’t yet: the paper form is still blank.
- **Not everything gets the same accuracy:** one carousel routine is at 40% accuracy and is being fixed. The overall figure doesn’t hide the exception.
